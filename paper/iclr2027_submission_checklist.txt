ICLR 2027 submission checklist
==============================

Deadlines (Anywhere on Earth)
----------------------------

Abstract: September 18, 2026, 11:59 PM AOE
Paper:    September 25, 2026, 11:59 PM AOE
Portal:   https://openreview.net/group?id=ICLR.cc/2027/Conference

Before the abstract deadline
----------------------------

[ ] Create or update an OpenReview profile for every author.
[ ] Use institutional email addresses where available; profile moderation
    without one can take up to two weeks.
[ ] Finalize the complete author list. No authors may be added after the
    abstract deadline.
[ ] Submit a genuine title and abstract suitable for reviewer bidding.
[ ] Suggested subject areas: reinforcement learning; datasets and benchmarks.
[ ] Suggested keywords: offline reinforcement learning; goal-conditioned
    reinforcement learning; dataset segmentation; truncation; reliability.
[ ] Confirm the submission quota and reciprocal-review eligibility shown on
    each author's OpenReview profile.

Before the paper deadline
-------------------------

[ ] Upload paper/main.pdf.
[ ] Upload dist/segbench-gc-anonymous.zip as supplementary code.
[ ] Keep the paper anonymous and leave \iclrfinalcopy commented out.
[ ] Confirm main text ends by page 9.
[ ] Confirm the appendix follows the references.
[ ] Copy the paper's AI-use disclosure into the submission form consistently.
[ ] Read and acknowledge the ICLR Code of Ethics and Code of Conduct.
[ ] Confirm the paper is not under review at another archival venue.
[ ] Verify all author profiles and author order; no author changes are allowed
    after the paper deadline.
[ ] Run:
      bash scripts/compile_paper.sh
      .venv/bin/python scripts/check_iclr_submission.py
      .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q
      .venv/bin/python scripts/build_anonymous_supplement.py

After abstract submission
-------------------------

[ ] Follow ICLR/OpenReview instructions for reviewer registration. ICLR states
    that submissions with no author registered as a reviewer can be desk
    rejected; first-time-author exemptions and quotas depend on profile data.
[ ] Monitor program-chair policy updates and OpenReview messages.
